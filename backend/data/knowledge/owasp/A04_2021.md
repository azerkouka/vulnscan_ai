# A04:2021 - Insecure Design

**Category:** Insecure Design
**URL:** https://owasp.org/Top10/A04_2021-Insecure_Design/

A new category for 2021, focusing on risks related to design and
architectural flaws, with a call for more use of threat modeling, secure
design patterns, and reference architectures. Insecure design is not the
source of all other Top 10 categories, but represents a broad category of
"missing or ineffective control design".

## Common weaknesses

- Missing business logic validation (e.g. an e-commerce checkout that does
  not verify that the total price cannot be negative).
- Trusting the client to enforce rules that must be enforced server-side.
- Failing to segregate tenants effectively in a multi-tenant architecture
  by design.
- Credential recovery workflows relying on "security questions" that are
  guessable or discoverable.

## How to prevent

- Establish and use a secure development lifecycle with security
  professionals to help evaluate and design security-related controls.
- Use threat modeling for critical authentication, access control,
  business logic, and key flows.
- Write unit and integration tests to validate that all critical flows are
  resistant to the threat model.
- Segregate tier layers on the system and network layers depending on the
  exposure and protection needs.
- Limit resource consumption by user or service.
